ChargeSentry – battery heat guard for Android

ChargeSentry watches your phone's battery temperature. If the battery overheats while charging, it switches off a Shelly smart plug to cut the power, and sounds an alarm. It also warns when the phone heats up without a charger.

Everything works on your phone and in your home network. ChargeSentry has no user accounts, no ads and no analytics.

ChargeSentry adds to the phone's own thermal protection; it does not replace it, and it is not a smoke or fire alarm.
