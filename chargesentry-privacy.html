ChargeSentry privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

In short

ChargeSentry does not collect, send or sell personal data. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking libraries. Everything it stores stays on your phone, and the only device it talks to is the Shelly smart plug that you link to it in your own home network. The developer receives no data from the app.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Battery temperature, battery level and charging state, at most one reading per minute, for the last 7 days.
  • An event log (warnings, alarms, charging cuts, plug connection events), the latest 500 events.
  • Your settings, such as temperature limits and alarm options.
  • The linked plug's network address, name and device ID.
  • The plug password, if your plug has one. It is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore of your phone.
  • A random installation ID created by the app.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What the app sends, and where

ChargeSentry communicates only with the Shelly plug you link, directly over your local Wi-Fi network. It does not send data over the internet to the developer or to anyone else. The app sends the plug:

  • commands to switch the power on or off and to set a safety timer;
  • a small watchdog script that switches the plug off if the phone stops responding;
  • the random installation ID and the device name of your phone (for example "Galaxy S25"), so that the plug knows which device controls it. They are stored only on your own plug and removed when you unlink the plug in the app.

To find plugs, the app searches your local network (mDNS). If you use Shelly's own app or cloud service, the plug's manufacturer handles that data under its own privacy policy; ChargeSentry does not use the Shelly cloud.

Exporting data

You can export the temperature history and event log as CSV files. This happens only when you choose it, and the files go only where you send them.

Google Play

Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Google may provide the developer with aggregated statistics and crash reports, as described in Google's own policies. These do not include the data the app stores on your phone.

Children

ChargeSentry is not directed at children.

Your rights and contact

Because the developer does not receive or hold your data, there is nothing on our side to access or delete. You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app. For questions, email jyjupu@protonmail.com.
